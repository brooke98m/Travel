Antibes & Juan-les-Pins

Where to drink and go out, from our Jetstream guide

Cocktails Late Night
Afternoon Cocktails
  • Quinto Cielo at The 1932
    Our hotel's rooftop bar. Mediterranean views, golden hour perfection, zero commute.
    Open daily, noon to 1:00am
  • Bar Fitzgerald at Hôtel Belles Rives
    Where F. Scott Fitzgerald lived and wrote. Art Deco glamour right on the water.
    Closes at 11:00pm
  • Plage la Jetée
    Beach bar with feet-in-the-sand cocktails on the Juan-les-Pins waterfront.
    Closes at 6:00pm
After Dinner & Late Night
  • Pam Pam
    The institution of Juan-les-Pins. Tiki cocktail show, live Brazilian dancers, funky glassware. Genuinely fun even if the cocktails run sweet. Budget for queues in season.
    Daily, 3:00pm to 4:30am
  • Epi Beach
    The smart play for a chic late drink with views, not chaos.
    Seasonal hours, call ahead
  • Villa Djunah
    Gardens, beach club, and late-night energy all in one. The Riviera version of a destination venue.
    Fri to Sat, 6:00pm to 2:00am
  • Le Village
    The proper club. Fri/Sat only with a real door policy (no shorts, no sneakers for guys). €20 entry includes a drink.
    Fri to Sat, midnight to 5:00am
  • L'Absinthe Bar
    The best left-field option in the area. Live music, the absinthe ritual is a great group activity, and it's open late every night. A 10-minute drive into Antibes old town but worth the detour.
    Daily until 2:30am
  • La Suite Wine Bar
    For an actual nightcap with grown-up energy. Wine-forward, low-key, and the right way to end a night.
    Mon to Sat, 6:30pm to 12:30am · Closed Sunday

Cannes

A few picks for the festival week, curated for us

Dinner Cocktails Late Night World Cup
Dinner
  • La Palme d'Or
    Two Michelin stars inside Hôtel Martinez, with a bay-view terrace. The special-occasion table on the Croisette. Book well ahead.
  • Da Laura
    The cult Italian everyone lines up for. No reservations, generous plates, worth the wait.
  • Astoux et Brun
    The classic seafood institution near the old port. Towering plateaux de fruits de mer, open late, always buzzing.
  • La Môme
    Mediterranean plates and a see-and-be-seen crowd. Peak festival-week energy.
  • Vesuvio
    The Croisette pizza and Italian institution. Reliable, lively, and open late.
  • Le Park 45
    Refined dining at the Grand Hôtel with a garden terrace set back from the boulevard.
  • Bobo Bistro
    Trendy, low-lit bistro up in Le Suquet. Great for a lively group dinner in the old town.
Cocktails & Rooftops
  • Le Panorama at JW Marriott
    360-degree rooftop over the sea and the hills, reopening for summer 2026. The view drink.
  • La Californie at Hôtel Martinez
    Chic and exclusive, with sweeping Croisette and sea views. Dress the part.
  • Le Roof at Five Seas Hotel
    Sleek rooftop with a pool and a stylish crowd. A sophisticated pre-dinner drink.
  • Sky Lounge at Radisson Blu 1835
    Sweeping views over the bay and the old port. An easy, less fussy rooftop option.
  • L'Amiral at Hôtel Martinez
    The classic piano bar. Proper cocktails, live music, old-Riviera glamour.
Late Night
  • Le Bâoli
    The legendary dinner-into-club on Port Canto. Starts as a restaurant, ends as the night out.
  • Gotha Club
    The big-name nightclub when you want a proper late one. Festival pop-ups and DJs.
  • La Folie Douce Cannes
    High-energy party with performers and a champagne-soaked crowd. Goes from afternoon to late.
  • Jimmy'z
    A polished club option for dancing without the chaos.
Where to Watch the World Cup

The 2026 World Cup runs through festival week, so matches will be on during our stay. These pubs put it on the big screens.

  • Morrison's Irish Pub
    The original Cannes pub, central, with six screens for every match. Live music most nights too.
  • The Quays
    On the old port, big screens, a classic spot to watch football with a Guinness.
  • Ma Nolan's
    Irish pub with live sport, food, and a reliably loud match-day crowd.
  • Station Tavern & Parry's Pub
    Two no-frills pubs by the train station that screen the big matches. Good backup if the Croisette spots are packed.
