    Gym, sauna + plunge Basement + back porch

    The pool table and gym in the basement are yours.

    Sauna

    Takes about 20 minutes to heat up, so start it first.

    1. Press the power button on the bottom right, below the screen.
    2. Tap the Sunlighten logo (white with an orange flower) in the top left, or press the center dot to find it.
    3. Pick a session. Quick Mode lets you set your own temp and time.
    4. Press Start Program. If it asks "Warm up?", set 20 minutes and come back.
    5. Put a towel on the bench. The center dot opens the menu for Netflix or YouTube.
    6. When you're done, wipe it down with the spray on the bench and a small towel.

    Cold plunge

    Outside on the back porch.

    1. Set the lid to the right, on top of the plunge.
    2. Check your ducky friend. He'll tell you how cold it is.
    3. Aim for 3 minutes. Put a song on!
    4. Close it up when you're done.
