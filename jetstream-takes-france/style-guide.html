For Her  ·  Riviera
Bring proper heels.
Several Riviera venues require heels for women. Pack at least two pairs (one strappy, one closed-toe stiletto) for evenings.
La Guérite Baoli Medusa Group Dinners
For Him  ·  Riviera
No hats indoors.
Several Riviera venues do not allow men to wear hats. Caps are great for the boat and beach. They come off the moment you step inside.
