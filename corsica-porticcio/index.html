Worth knowing

Buy shuttle tickets one way at a time

A return costs €8 but you have to choose your sailing when you buy, and it can't be changed. Two singles at €5 each cost €2 more per person and let you leave when you feel like it.

Monday is statistically the driest day of the month

28 September averages the least rain and the lowest humidity of any day in September in Ajaccio. Pleasant coincidence, not a forecast.

But late September can turn

Highs sit around 22°C / 73°F, lows near 18°C / 63°F, and the sea holds about 23°C. September averages 45mm of rain and October jumps to 90mm, so you're arriving at the hinge. Pack one warm layer for the boat and evenings, and a light rain jacket.

Ajaccio shuts on Mondays

Le Don Ba', La Mostra and most of the good kitchens in town are closed. That's why Monday stays on this side of the water.
