How a night actually stacks

Local hours, not American ones
19:00
Apéro

Rooftops peak now. Sunset is 21:50 in June, 20:00 in early September, 17:00 in December.

21:00
Dinner

The real second seating. A 19:30 booking marks you as a tourist and gets you the bad table.

23:30
Bars

Pigalle and the Marais fill. Most cocktail rooms run to 02:00, a few to 04:00.

01:00
Floors open

Clubs and salsa socials do not really start before this. Last métro is 01:15, or 02:15 Friday and Saturday.

05:30
First métro

Between those two, it is Noctilien buses or a cab. Book the cab in the app before you walk out.

Nights that string together

Five routes, all walkable end to end
8e / 16e

The avenue

  1. L'Avenue for a 19:00 drink on the terrace
  2. Gigi upstairs at 21:00, stay for the music
  3. L'Arc or Costes after midnight
3e / 4e

The Marais

  1. Terraza Mikuna at sunset on the BHV roof
  2. Les Enfants du Marché standing, early
  3. Candelaria through the back door until two
9e / 18e

Pigalle

  1. Perruche for the view, ten minutes downhill
  2. Dirty Dick by 20:00, before it fills
  3. Lulu White, then Chez Moune in the basement
4e / 11e

Bastille

  1. Bonnie for the glassed-in view and dinner
  2. Le Balajo on a Thursday for the SBK night
  3. Ave Maria if you want to keep going
5e / 6e / 14e

The salsa run

  1. Quais de Seine Sunday at 18:00, free, outdoors
  2. Cubana Café after, twenty minutes west
  3. La Pachanga Tuesday to Friday for the real floor

More, if there is room

Not mapped, still worth it

Cocktails

Little Red Door
60 rue Charlot, 3e. On every world's-best list, and it deserves it. Book online.
Bar Hemingway
The Ritz, 1er. Fifty seats, no reservations. Be at the door at 18:00 or forget it.
Danico
Galerie Vivienne, 2e. Through the back of a pasta bar, inside a covered arcade from 1823.
Le Syndicat
51 rue du Faubourg Saint-Denis, 10e. French spirits only. The scruffy facade is on purpose.
Moonshiner
5 rue Sedaine, 11e. Walk through the pizzeria and open the freezer door.
Lulu White
12 rue Frochot, 9e. Absinthe bar, same team as Dirty Dick, two doors down.

Views and tables

Créatures
Galeries Lafayette roof, 9e. The other department store rooftop, more garden and less scene than Perruche.
Madame Rêve
48 rue du Louvre, 1er. Terrace over the old central post office, looking at Saint-Eustache.
Le Perchoir Ménilmontant
14 rue Crespin du Gast, 11e. The original Paris rooftop. Queue, no bookings, worth it in summer.
Clamato and Septime
80 rue de Charonne, 11e. Clamato takes walk-ins. Septime opens bookings three weeks out at 10:00 sharp.
Chez Janou
2 rue Roger Verlomme, 3e. Provençal, packed, and the chocolate mousse arrives as a whole bowl.
Bambino
25 rue Amelot, 11e. Natural wine, small plates, vinyl. Standing room by 22:00.

Late

Silencio des Prés
50 rue Dauphine, 6e. David Lynch designed the original. Members until midnight, then open to everyone.
Chez Moune
54 rue Jean-Baptiste Pigalle, 9e. A 1936 lesbian cabaret turned basement club. Mirrored, small, sweaty.
Le Comptoir Général
80 quai de Jemmapes, 10e. Down a courtyard off the canal. Afro-Caribbean bar, ginger punch, occasional Latin nights.
Nouveau Casino
109 rue Oberkampf, 11e. Live room next to Café Charbon that turns into a club after the set.

Before you book

Season

Rooftop season runs April to October. June and September are the two best months in Paris. August empties out and a lot of good kitchens close for three weeks.

Fashion week

Late September and early March make the 8th arrondissement unbookable. L'Avenue, Costes and Gigi go to invitation and regulars only.

Doors

At L'Arc and Bonnie, a dinner reservation is the way in. Turning up at the club door at 01:00 without a table or a name is how you stand outside.

Salsa nights move

Paris socials shift venue and night more than most cities. Check salsa-paris.fr or the venue's own page the week you land, not before.

Shoes

The Quais floor is stone and the Balajo floor is old parquet. Bring something with a suede or leather sole and leave the rubber at home.

Cash

The outdoor Quais dances run on donations in a hat. Most bars are card, but carry twenty euros for the riverbank.
